      How these transcriptions work

      Each document is encoded in TEI-XML. The Reading view expands abbreviations and applies corrections. The Diplomatic view shows the text as written, including deletions and original spellings. Every document links to the archive that holds the original.

      Are you a community member, or do you hold rights in one of these records? You can request a correction or removal.
